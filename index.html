Based on my bachelor's thesis

Fake news detection with machine learning algorithms

Batuhan Battal, Başar Yıldırım, Ömer Faruk Dinçaslan and Gulay Cicek

We trained and compared classifiers that tell fake news from real news on social media. The dataset has about 42,000 labeled examples. We removed numbers, stop words and HTML tags, then compared single and ensemble models.

Random forest performed best, with 76% accuracy. Every model was checked with 5-fold cross-validation, so the comparison doesn't depend on one lucky split.
